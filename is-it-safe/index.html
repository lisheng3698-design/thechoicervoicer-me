Is The Choicer Voicer safe to use?

On thechoicervoicer.me, microphone access starts only after your action, takes stay in the current tab for local replay and scoring, and no account is required. The site also uses consent-controlled analytics and third-party ads, which deserve a clear disclosure.

Short answer

Use it with normal browser precautions.

This assessment covers this domain and its current browser implementation. It does not certify similarly named sites, downloads, extensions, or external destinations.

Microphone

Permission is requested after a button press. A take is processed in the tab and is not intentionally uploaded by the game.

Account and storage

No sign-in is required. Gameplay state and recordings are temporary to the current browser session.

Third parties

Analytics activates according to consent. Production pages can load Adsterra ads and user-opened external links.

What actually happens

Microphone permission is scoped by your browser

The game cannot listen until you press the microphone-check control and the browser grants access. The live level meter confirms that an input is available. During a round, the browser creates a temporary take for replay and scoring; the game does not intentionally provide that microphone audio to Analytics or advertising integrations.

Recordings and scores are local to the active tab

A take is held in browser memory while you review or score it. Closing or reloading the tab ends that gameplay session, and there is no account library or cloud recording history on this site. Local processing reduces exposure, but you should still avoid speaking private information into any microphone-based game.

Analytics is consent-controlled

The site offers an analytics choice. Before consent, analytics storage and ad personalization are denied by the site configuration. If analytics is accepted, Google Analytics can receive page and interaction events such as starting a game or changing language; it is not intentionally sent microphone audio.

Advertising is a separate third-party surface

Production pages may load Adsterra advertising, including formats that can open an advertiser destination. Ad providers may process browser, device, network, cookie, and ad-interaction information under their own terms. Ads are not gameplay instructions, and an advertiser page is outside this site. Do not install unknown software, grant notification access, or enter sensitive information merely because an ad asks.

External references leave this domain

Some content credits and source references link to other websites. Check the destination domain before continuing. A link documents a source; it does not mean this site controls that destination or endorses every page it may later show.

Dialogue content follows a documented rights boundary

The playable pack uses original, cleared, or public-domain source material documented in the local scene manifest. It does not package commercial movie dialogue. The browser implementation, interface, scoring, and audio assets are maintained as an independent project.

Safer-use checklist

Keep control in your hands

  • Confirm the address is thechoicervoicer.me
  • Grant mic access only while playing
  • Remove permission after the session if desired
  • Never say passwords or private details
  • Treat ads and external links as separate sites
  • Review your analytics choice
