How to play

One round takes about ten seconds.

The 16-scene library mixes four original film-style performances with 12 public-domain world classics. Each show draws four scenes, and every scene displays its country, source, character, original voice profile, context, and rights status.

1

Test the mic

Grant browser permission and watch the live level meter. Audio begins only after your click.

2

Listen and perform

Replay the original performance if needed, then record one timed delivery of the line.

3

Replay and score

Compare both performances, reveal five votes and five one-word reviews, then continue through all four scenes.

Fairness notes

What the score actually measures

This browser game is a playful similarity meter, not a singing exam or identity detector. The timing component compares recording length with the reference. Energy scoring follows the loud–soft shape. Pitch scoring compares the direction and spacing of voiced notes while largely ignoring whether your natural voice starts high or low.

Background noise, laptop audio leaking into the microphone, aggressive noise suppression, and very quiet input can affect the result. Headphones and a normal speaking distance give the cleanest comparison. A score is entertainment, not a judgment of voice quality.

Game FAQ

Where does my recording go?

It stays inside this browser tab as a temporary object URL. There is no upload request, database, or user account. Choose “End mic” or close the tab to stop the stream and discard playback.

Why is the record button locked?

Listen to the reference once first. If audio does not play, turn off silent mode, raise media volume, and retry. Embedded in-app browsers may restrict audio and microphone APIs.

Who built this browser voice game?

This browser edition uses original code, interface, scoring, and AI-designed spoken performances created for thechoicervoicer.me.
