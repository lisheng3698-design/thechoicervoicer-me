Privacy policy

The current browser challenge is designed so your recorded voice remains on your device.

Microphone access

The game asks for microphone permission only after you press the soundcheck button. The browser provides a live media stream to in-page Web Audio and MediaRecorder APIs. Signal measurements are computed locally.

Recordings

A take becomes a temporary browser object URL so you can replay it. The site has no audio-upload endpoint and does not transmit or save the recording to a server. Ending the mic session or closing the tab stops the stream and discards the temporary playback object.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

The site has no user accounts, forms that collect personal details, or advertising scripts. It uses Google Analytics 4 only after loading privacy-preserving consent defaults. Analytics storage is denied unless you choose “Allow analytics.” If you decline, the preference is saved in your browser and no analytics cookies are set by this site.

When analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may receive the page URL, approximate location derived from network information, browser and device details, timestamps, and privacy-safe game actions such as starting or completing a round. The site does not send microphone audio, temporary recordings, player names, or text you enter. Advertising storage, ad personalization, and ad-user-data consent remain denied.

You can review the saved choice at any time. Google processes Analytics data under its own privacy policy.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamps for security and delivery. This is separate from microphone audio, which is not part of page requests.

Children

The site does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. A parent or guardian should control browser permissions for younger players.

Changes

If uploads, accounts, advertising, or other data collection are added, this policy must be updated before those features go live.
