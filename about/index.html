About

About the browser voice game.

thechoicervoicer.me is an independently developed browser game and gameplay guide. No third-party ownership, sponsorship, endorsement, or operation is claimed.

What this site does

The site provides a directly playable voice challenge, a transparent explanation of local scoring, microphone troubleshooting, privacy information, and bilingual gameplay guidance.

Originality and attribution

The browser challenge uses independent TypeScript, Web Audio code, Canvas visuals, interface design, scoring logic, and purpose-made spoken performances. It does not bundle third-party game files, reproduce another interface, or copy community voice packs.

Content standard

Every bundled scene records its source, character, context, country, voice profile, and rights status. New content must be original, public domain, or supplied under documented compatible permission.
